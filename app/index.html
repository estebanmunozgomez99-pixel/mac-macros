Welcome to Mac Macros

Track what you eat on campus in a few taps. Every dining hall and campus restaurant is already in here.

  • 🔍 Search, 🎤 say it or ▦ scan it. "Medium protein latte from Tims" works. So does the barcode on a granola bar.
  • 🍽️ Make it a meal. Add your sides right away, or build your own bowl, pasta or omelette.
  • 🎯 Set a goal, 📈 see your progress. Each day gets a score for how close you landed.

Mac Macros is a student project and isn't affiliated with McMaster University or any restaurant. Numbers come from published nutrition info and some are estimates. It's not medical advice. Your data stays on this phone.
