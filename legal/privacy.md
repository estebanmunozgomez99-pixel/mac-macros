# Maroon Privacy Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

Maroon is a free macro tracker for McMaster University students, made by one student, [OWNER NAME], in Hamilton, Ontario, Canada. This policy explains what Maroon does with your information. The short version: **your food log stays on your device, and nothing about you is sent to me.**

## The short version

- No accounts. You never give Maroon your name, email or phone number.
- Your food log, goals and settings are stored only on this device, in your browser's storage.
- No analytics, no tracking, no ads, and no selling or sharing of data.
- Maroon never records or keeps audio, and never keeps what you said.

## What Maroon stores, and where

Everything below is saved only in your browser's local storage on this device. I can't see it, and it isn't sent to me or to any server I run (I don't run one).

- **Food log:** what you logged, on which day, servings and any extras.
- **Goals:** your daily calorie, protein, carb and fat goals, and the goals each day was scored against.
- **Goal calculator details (optional):** if you use "Calculate my goals", the sex, age, height, weight, activity level and priority you entered, so the form is filled in next time.
- **Recent foods and My foods:** foods you logged recently, and foods you scanned, typed in or built yourself.
- **Settings:** light or dark appearance, whether you've seen the welcome tour and the privacy notice.
- **Downloaded menu data and opening hours:** a copy of the latest menu and campus hours, so the app works offline.

If you uninstall the app or clear this site's data in your browser, all of this is deleted.

## Cookies and storage

Maroon doesn't use cookies. It uses your browser's local storage only to make the app work: to remember your log, goals and settings between visits. There is no tracking storage, so Maroon doesn't need a cookie banner.

## When Maroon uses the internet

Maroon only connects to the internet for these things:

- **Loading the app, menu updates and opening hours.** Maroon is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When your device downloads the app, a newer menu or the latest opening hours, GitHub may keep standard server logs such as your IP address and browser type. These requests contain nothing about your food log. See GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
- **Barcode lookups (only if you use Scan).** The barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a non-profit food database, to look up the product. Nothing else is sent. See https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy
- **Speak (only if you use it).** Maroon uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. While the microphone is on, your browser may send the audio to its own speech service to turn it into text: Google for Chrome and most Android browsers, Apple for Safari. That is between you and your browser's maker, under their privacy policies. Maroon only receives the resulting text, uses it right away to find matching foods, and doesn't store the audio or the text. The microphone is only used after you tap Speak, and you can turn off microphone access at any time in your device settings.
- **Camera (only if you use Scan).** The camera picture is read on your device to find a barcode. Images are never saved or sent anywhere.
- **Emailing me (only if you choose to).** "Report a problem" opens your own email app with a message filled in. If you send it, I receive your email address and whatever is in the message. I only use it to reply and to fix the app, and I don't add you to any mailing list.

## What Maroon doesn't do

- No accounts, sign-ins or profiles on a server.
- No analytics, tracking pixels, advertising or fingerprinting.
- No location tracking.
- No selling, renting or sharing of personal information.
- No recording or storing of audio or speech transcripts.

## Your choices: export and delete

- **Export my data:** Settings → Data → Export my data saves a file (JSON) with your log, goals, foods and settings. You can keep it as a backup or restore it on another device.
- **Delete all my data:** Settings → Data → Delete all my data asks you to confirm, then permanently erases everything Maroon stored on this device and resets the app. It can't be undone, so export first if you want a copy.
- You can also clear this site's data from your browser settings at any time.

## Children

Maroon is made for university students. It isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Because nothing you enter leaves your device, there's no personal information on my side to collect.

## Health information

Nutrition numbers in Maroon come from published nutrition information, and some are estimates. Maroon is for general information only and isn't medical, dietary or nutrition advice.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the effective date at the top. If a change affects what happens to your information, the app will show a notice before it takes effect.
