Mac Macros Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

The short version
  • There are no accounts. We don't collect your name, email or any other personal information.
  • Everything you log (meals, goals, body measurements for the goal calculator, saved foods) stays on your phone.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell or share data.
  • The only times the app talks to the internet are listed below: checking for menu updates, looking up a barcode, and turning speech into text if you use the microphone.

What stays on your phone

Your food log, daily goals, goal-calculator details (sex, age, height, weight, activity level, priority), saved and scanned foods, and your progress scores are stored only in the app's storage on your device. We (the Mac Macros team) have no server that receives this information and cannot see it. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it, unless you saved a backup.

Backups: "Save a backup" in Settings creates a file on your device. You choose where it goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive). That file is yours; we never receive it.

When the app uses the internet

FeatureWhat is sentTo whom
Menu updatesA normal request for the latest menu file. Nothing about you or your log is included. GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which hosts the menu file. GitHub may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement.
Barcode lookupOnly the barcode number of the product you scanned or typed. Open Food Facts, a non-profit food database.
Voice logging (microphone)What you say, only while the microphone button is on, to turn it into text. Your phone's built-in speech recognition. Depending on your device, this may be processed by Apple (iPhone) or Google (Android) under their privacy policies. The app itself never records or stores audio; only the resulting text is used, on your phone.

Camera and microphone

The camera is used only to read barcodes while the scanner is open. Images are processed on your phone and are never saved or sent anywhere. The microphone is used only while you're using voice logging. You can turn either permission off at any time in your phone's settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

Children

Mac Macros is made for university students and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Health information

Nutrition numbers in the app come from published nutrition information and some are estimates. Mac Macros is for general information only and isn't medical or nutrition advice.

Not affiliated

Mac Macros is an independent student project and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, McMaster University or any restaurant named in the app.
