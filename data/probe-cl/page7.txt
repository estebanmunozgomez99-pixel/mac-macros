Gluten-Friendly
Options Without
Compromise 

Eating gluten-free should never mean sacrificing flavour. That’s why Chopped Leaf offers a variety of gluten-friendly dining options made with fresh produce, lean proteins, and signature dressings. From salads to bowls, our gluten-friendly menu items are crafted to provide nutritious, satisfying meals without compromise.  

Any salad or bowl can be made gluten friendly with the removal of pita chips, croutons & chow mien noodles.  

Examples:  

Bangkok salad- remove chow mien noodles and pita chip 
Signature salad- remove pita chip and croutons 
Caesar salad- remove croutons and pita chip ​

Gluten friendly proteins/ add-ons:
Chicken, Sirloin Steak, Tuna, Falafel, Eggs, Shrimp, Quinoa, Brown Basmati rice 

The Harvest, Sunshine, Cobb and Goddess bowls are naturally gluten friendly, for salads just remove the pita chip!

A Variety of Protein Options

Protein plays an essential role in a balanced diet, helping to support muscle repair, sustain energy levels, and keep you feeling full and satisfied throughout the day. At Chopped Leaf, it’s easy to enjoy high protein meals with a variety of healthy protein options that can be added to any salad, bowl, or wrap. Our proteins are prepared to complement our fresh and flavourful choppings, to help you reach your protein goals on-the-go. No matter your dietary preference or nutrition goals, you can customize your meal with the protein that works best for you. 

Gluten Friendly Protein options: Seasoned Grilled Chicken, Sirloin Steak, Tuna, Falafel, Eggs, and Shrimp.  

Vegan Protein options: Falafel and Tofu.
