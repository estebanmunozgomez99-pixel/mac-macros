Delivery

Bringing fresh eats to your door.

Looking for local catering delivery that’s fresh, fast, and reliable? Chopped Leaf offers catering delivery near you, so your event runs smoothly and deliciously. Whether you’re ordering for 10 or 100, we’ll deliver made-to-order meals crafted with real ingredients and bold flavours.

Pick Up

Pick up freshly-made catering on your schedule.​

Prefer to pick it up? Order ahead and swing by your nearest Chopped Leaf location for fast, convenient catering pickup. It’s the perfect solution for smaller gatherings or when you’re short on time but big on flavour.
