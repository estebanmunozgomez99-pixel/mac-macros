Get excited
for greens.

We believe greens should be as crave-worthy as the comfort foods you love most.

Here's why…

Fresh Choppings.
Bold Colours.
Real Flavour.

Our meals start with fresh, whole ingredients delivered raw and unprocessed to every location. From there, our team hand-chops every topping daily ensuring your greens are bright, flavourful, and made to order. These fresh chopping’s bring colour, crunch, and quality to every salad, bowl, and wrap. Taste the difference real, hand-prepped ingredients make in every bite.

Irresistible
Signature
Dressings.

Our signature dressings take your chopping’s to the next level and are only available at the Chopped Leaf. Using fresh, high-quality ingredients, each blend adds a bold burst of flavour to every bite. Whether you love a zesty vinaigrette, a creamy ranch, or a tangy balsamic, we have got a dressing that is made to compliment your meal perfectly. We apologize in advance, getting hooked is a part of the experience.

One Menu,
Made for You.

From fresh and light to bold and filling, our menu offers flavour-packed creations that can be enjoyed like healthy salads, wraps or bowls. We also offer comforting soups, toasted quesadillas, tasty sandwiches and more.

With plenty of vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free and fully customizable options, it’s easy to build a meal that fits your lifestyle. Whether you switch it up every visit or stick to your favourite, there’s always something crave-worthy on the menu.

Fruit
Infused
Chopped
Water.

Made fresh daily, our signature “Chopped Water” is infused with fresh lemon and lime, featuring seasonal fruit, which is muddled for a naturally flavoured, refreshing drink. It’s the perfect way to stay hydrated and ditch sugary drinks, all while enjoying every ice-cold, delicious drop.

Feel Good
After You Eat.

Traditional comfort food might hit the spot in the moment, but it doesn’t always leave you feeling your best afterward. Our comfort greens deliver all the satisfaction your tastebuds crave, without the sluggish, heavy feeling that usually follows.

Packed with flavour and full of nutritious ingredients, our meals are designed to leave you feeling energized – physically and mentally. Next time you’re grabbing lunch, skip the traditional fast food and choose something that tastes good and makes you feel even better.
