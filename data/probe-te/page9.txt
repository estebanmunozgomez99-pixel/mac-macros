Founded in Canada in 1986, we've been cooking up fresh & tasty teriyaki meals on our flat top grill for over 35 years. Since the opening of our very first mall location, we have flourished, expanding our reach to serve guests in both food courts and storefront restaurants, as well as universities & hospitals. This journey of growth has been fueled by our dedication to quality, hospitality, and the joy of sharing delicious Japanese food with our valued customers.

MISSION

To provide guests with a flavourful and memorable, made before your eyes, authentic Japanese food experience.

VISION

To be recognized as the leading destination in Canada for authentic and fresh teriyaki cuisine. Known for our commitment to excellence for taste, quality and service.

VALUES

Authenticity, Community Engagement, Welcoming, Consistent, Quality, Innovative.

OUR COMMITMENT

We're committed to freshness, quality, and providing an unforgettable dining experience. Our teppanyaki chefs to create memorable moments and meaningful connections with each and every one of our guests.
