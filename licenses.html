Maroon Licenses and Credits

Maroon is built from original code plus a few open-source pieces. Their licenses are below.

Logo

The "bitten M" logo is an original design made for Maroon. No McMaster University crest, logos or "Marauders" marks are used, and no restaurant logos are used.

Fonts

  • Figtree by Erik Kennedy, SIL Open Font License 1.1. Bundled with the app (no font is downloaded from Google or anywhere else). Full license below.
  • The marketing font Horizon (Fontense) is not used in the app.

Icons

  • The flame, drumstick, wheat and drop icons are adapted from Lucide (ISC License, below). Other icons are drawn for Maroon.

Barcode reader

Used only on devices without a built-in barcode reader.

  • barcode-detector 3.2.2 by Ze-Zheng Wu, MIT License (below).
  • zxing-wasm 3.1.3 by Ze-Zheng Wu, MIT License (below). It includes ZXing-C++, Apache License 2.0: https://github.com/zxing-cpp/zxing-cpp/blob/master/LICENSE

Data

  • McMaster Hospitality Services menu nutrition (macnutrition.mcmaster.ca) and opening hours (hospitality.mcmaster.ca). Used with permission from McMaster Hospitality Services.
  • Published nutrition information from Tim Hortons, Second Cup, Chopped Leaf, Booster Juice, Pizza Pizza, Starbucks and Teriyaki Experience, and posted menu-board calories from Paramount Lebanese Kitchen (protein, carbs and fat for Paramount are estimates). Brand names belong to their owners and are used only to identify where food comes from.
  • Barcode lookups: product data from Open Food Facts (https://world.openfoodfacts.org), available under the Open Database License (ODbL); product images and text under their own licenses.

The full list of sources with dates is in the app under Settings → Data sources.
